Install it — about twenty seconds

Do this once. After that it opens instantly from your home screen and works in classrooms with no signal.

iPhone / iPad
  1. Open the app link in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen
Android
  1. Open the app link in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu
  3. Tap Install app
Laptop
  1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar
  3. It opens in its own window
Lost signal mid-observation? Nothing happens. The app saves to your phone first, finishes the walkthrough normally, and sends itself the next time you have a connection. The home screen tells you if anything is still waiting.

How a visit works

Tap a name, pick your mode, and go. Nothing is required — rate what you saw.

Two modes

Walkthrough (3–5 min) — choose one or two focus dimensions and only those indicators appear. Your choice is remembered, so if the building focus this month is Agency, every walkthrough opens there.

Full ATOT (20 min) — all five dimensions, all 23 indicators.

The seven sections

ATOT — tap a dimension, then 1–4 on each indicator, or — for not observed.

Actions — teacher on the left, students on the right. Tap what you see, tap again to count it twice. A live engagement percentage appears as you go — the fastest read on who is doing the cognitive work.

Room, Systems, Safety — tap once for yes, twice for no, three times to clear.

Notes — timestamped against the visit clock.

Wrap up — shows your own highest and lowest ratings from this visit, then asks for a strength, a growth area, and a next step.

Two things worth knowing

Blank is never a zero. Anything you leave alone is recorded as "not observed" and left out of the averages entirely.

Anything you mark No on the room, systems, or safety lists is collected into a flagged list — so a burned-out projector and a blocked exit surface through the same walkthrough instead of needing a separate process.

The rating scale

RatingMeaningHow much of the class
4Very Evidentmore than 75% of learners
3Evident51–74% of learners
2Somewhat Evident25–50% of learners
1Not Evidentless than 25% of learners

ATOT also asks you to weigh three factors on every indicator: implementation (done with quality and fidelity), authenticity (embedded in the room's routine, not staged for the visit), and effect on learners (did it change what students actually did). The app collapses these into one rating for speed — hold all three in mind when you pick the number.

Questions

A locally built tool aligned to the Alabama Teacher Observation Tool. Not affiliated with, produced by, or endorsed by the Alabama State Department of Education. Official ATGP observations are still entered in the state ATOT platform — this is for the frequent walkthroughs in between. Questions about the state program go to ATGP@alsde.edu.

Observation records describe adults. Do not enter student names, ID numbers, or anything that identifies an individual learner — the app will stop you if you try.
